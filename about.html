About Stanrod

A small shop built around useful work

Stanrod Welding & Plasma is a hands-on Cheyenne shop. The business grew around custom fabrication, repairs, plasma cutting, metal art, and the kind of odd jobs that usually start with, “I’m not sure how to fix this, but…”

Meet the owner

Adam Stanfill

Adam owns and operates Stanrod Welding & Plasma in Cheyenne. He is usually the person who answers your text, looks at your photos, works through the problem with you, and then builds or repairs the piece in the shop or on site.

Stanrod has grown for more than a decade by taking on the work that does not always fit neatly into a catalog: custom fabrication, mobile repairs, CNC plasma work, structural details, equipment modifications, one-off prototypes, and finished metal art.

Adam's approach is simple: understand what the customer actually needs first, then choose the fabrication method that gets there cleanly and practically. The tools matter, but the problem solving is what customers are really hiring.

The idea

Help first. Fabricate second.

The real value is not just owning welders, a plasma table, or a pile of steel. It is being able to look at a problem, understand what you are trying to accomplish, and build a practical way forward — whether that means helping a contractor close out a difficult item, giving a homeowner a custom piece that actually fits the space, or repairing equipment that cannot simply be replaced that afternoon.
