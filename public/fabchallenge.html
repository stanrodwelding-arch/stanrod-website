The process

FOUR STEPS. REAL WORK.

No quiz can tell you whether someone can fit, weld, finish, and deliver a clean fabrication. FabChallenge makes the work the test.

Choose a kit

Pick practice pieces, graded pieces, or a combination based on how much repetition you want.

Build it

Follow the supplied drawing, tolerances, and fabrication requirements using your own tools.

Submit it

Graded builds come back to Stanrod for inspection, scoring, and documentation.

Know your level

Get a result based on measurable fabrication quality — not a vague opinion.

What gets judged

THE BUILD HAS TO WORK — AND LOOK LIKE IT BELONGS IN A SHOP.

The assessment can account for fit-up, dimensional accuracy, weld quality, finishing, overall presentation, and whether the final assembly is safe, installable, powder-coat ready, and saleable.

Current working standard: 90/100 to pass. Final public wording will be matched to the exact grading packet before launch.
