More work from Stanrod

Five more projects. Five very different problems.

From residential work to field fabrication and roadside repair, these are more examples of the range of jobs that come through Stanrod.

Custom residential

Rawlins residential pergola

This was a residential pergola we did for a client in Rawlins, Wyoming. It was a unique mix of steel, aluminum, and a very special wet hot-rod paint. We also did a very custom gate to pull everything together.

Field fabrication

Wind turbine project

This job took Stanrod out into the field for heavy fabrication work around wind-energy equipment. It is a good example of the kind of work that does not arrive at the shop on a convenient little trailer — sometimes the shop has to go to the job.

One-off fabrication

Burning Man fire truck

This is a fire truck we heavily modified to go out to the Burning Man festival. We modified the spiral staircase on the back, added pop-up and down handrail, a pergola, and CNC-cut panels.

Outside Laramie

Ranch gate

This was a simple ranch gate we did just outside of Laramie. The gate itself was straightforward; the hard part was the site. The ground was too rocky to drill post holes, so we built it around clean, colored-concrete foundations instead.

Roadside repair

Oversized load back on the road

This oversized load broke down after managing to break 1-inch-thick steel. We cut new brackets, welded everything back in perfectly straight, and got the truck and its load back on the road.
