About Stanrod

A small shop built around useful work

Stanrod Welding & Plasma is a hands-on Cheyenne shop. The business grew around custom fabrication, repairs, plasma cutting, metal art, and the kind of odd jobs that usually start with, “I’m not sure how to fix this, but…”

The idea

Help first. Fabricate second.

The real value is not just owning welders, a plasma table, or a pile of steel. It is being able to look at a problem, understand what the customer is trying to accomplish, and build a practical way forward.

That might mean helping a contractor close out a difficult item, giving a homeowner a custom piece that actually fits the space, or repairing equipment that cannot simply be replaced that afternoon.

The work can be serious without the company feeling stiff. This is still a real shop, run by real people, with sparks, dirty floors, unusual projects, and the occasional shop-cat inspection.

Sometimes the project is personal

The reason behind a piece matters too

The wildlife piece shown here was made at no charge for a grieving family Adam has known since childhood. The animals were symbolic, the family helped design it, and Stanrod's job was to turn that meaning into metal.

A milestone for the shop

The first cut in a shop of our own

Adam and Megan are standing in front of the CNC plasma table as it made its first cut in a shop they owned instead of rented. It is one of those small moments that says a lot about how the business has grown.
