A few real jobs

This is the kind of work that comes through the door

These are the actual stories behind three very different Stanrod projects.

City of Laramie wayfinding

We had the privilege of installing 40 of these wayfinding signs for the City of Laramie. This one is out at the Laramie Regional Airport to direct visitors to Laramie or Centennial.

University of Wyoming College of Law

The main donor recognition wall was made entirely out of aluminum and still weighed over 500 lbs. Adam was given only four days to turn the final rendering into reality and have it installed, and he had to travel out of state just to get the materials.

A repair in the middle of nowhere

We were called out about two hours south of Laramie to repair this massive logging machine after it sheared off a few brackets. Not knowing what was broken before he got there, Adam brought steel stock so he could custom-build what was needed on the fly.
