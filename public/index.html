A few different kinds of jobs

There is not one “typical” Stanrod project

A production order, a trailer repair, and licensed metal art all use different parts of the shop.

Repeat recognition pieces

This was a mass-order project for a large company recognizing all the employees who helped on a large project. Each aluminum Steamboat got 1-inch offsets on the back, powder coat, and engraving. This was one stack of four.

Trailer repair

We have repaired dozens of trailers over the years, of all shapes and sizes. Quality is very important to us, so often when we see expanded metal we go through and fix any broken welds and weld each diamond for extra strength, which is what Adam is doing on this one.

Licensed Alabama signs

We hold an official license from the University of Alabama, and we love to make their signs out of solid aluminum and put the design in powder coat. Here Adam shows the two best-selling colors, Crimson Red and Hammer Black.
