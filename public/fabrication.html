Three kinds of work

Different customers. Same problem-solving mindset.

These are real Stanrod projects from the shop and field, with the actual stories behind them.

Contractor and commercial work

We had the privilege of installing 40 of these wayfinding signs for the City of Laramie. This one is out at the Laramie Regional Airport to direct visitors to Laramie or Centennial.

Homeowner projects

A large custom handrail done for a handicapped family in Cheyenne. This design is identical to the FabChallenge: 1½-inch square tube and ½-inch round pickets. Proof that if you can do the FabChallenge, you can make money with that skill.

Repairs and field work

We were called out to the middle of nowhere, about two hours south of Laramie, to repair a massive logging machine that had sheared off a few brackets. Adam brought steel stock so he could custom-build what was needed on the fly.

Public installation

Downtown Laramie

Custom DOWNTOWN LARAMIE sign installed by the historic footbridge in Laramie, Wyoming. Done in the traditional marquee style and Western font and powder coated to last, you can still visit it to this day.

University of Wyoming College of Law

Four days from rendering to reality

The main donor recognition wall at the University of Wyoming College of Law was made entirely out of aluminum and still weighed over 500 lbs. Adam was given only four days to turn the final rendering into reality and have it installed, and he had to travel out of state to even get the materials.

Built for the load

Water-treatment equipment platforms

Four of these were ordered to withstand 1,000-lb loads and had fully adjustable legs to accommodate water-treatment equipment in the basement of a church in Cheyenne, Wyoming.

Traditional plasma-cut work

Signs shipped worldwide

We have done countless traditional plasma-cut signs as well and shipped them worldwide. We powder coat each of them in the customer's choice of color right here in our shop. This Lake House sign was finished in Federal Standard Satin Black.

Working with other fabricators

Bringing power and welding to the site

We work with other welders and artists as well. Here we were providing mobile power and welding capability to Black Row Productions as they mounted one of their custom address signs into a large boulder.

Truck and equipment fitting

Service-bed fit-up

This job was custom fitting a service bed onto a stock truck. Many customers use us for this service because we have the heavy equipment to make it easy, and the CNC plasma and mobile welding rig both make the work go much faster.
