Three different problems

Fabrication is usually about figuring out what the job actually needs

These are three very different examples from the shop. None of them started with the same answer.

Heavy brackets built for a real load

When an off-the-shelf bracket will not carry the load or fit the structure, we can build one around the job. These 3/8-inch plate brackets were made to bolt to concrete walls and support W-beams for a ranch ATV bridge.

Custom signs built for your place

If you want a sign that fits the property instead of looking mass-produced, we can cut it, finish it, and powder coat it in-house. This Lake House sign was finished in Federal Standard Satin Black.

Make the equipment fit the truck

Service beds and work bodies do not always land on a stock chassis the way they should. We can cut, position, modify, and weld the mounts so the body fits the truck correctly and can get back to work.

Public installation

Downtown Laramie

Custom DOWNTOWN LARAMIE sign installed by the historic footbridge in Laramie, Wyoming. Done in the traditional marquee style and Western font and powder coated to last, you can still visit it to this day.

Built for the load

Adjustable Equipment Platforms

When heavy equipment has to sit level on an imperfect floor, the support underneath it matters. We built four steel platforms rated for 1,000-lb loads with fully adjustable legs so water-treatment equipment could be installed securely in a Cheyenne church basement.

Mobile capability

When the work cannot come to the shop

Some installs need power, welding, and fabrication capability right where the project sits. Here we supported another fabricator on site while a custom address sign was mounted into a large boulder.
